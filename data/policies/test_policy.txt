POLICYFORGE ACCEPTABLE USE & SECURITY POLICY

1. PHISHING
Employees must not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
Employees should report suspicious emails to the IT team.

2. PASSWORDS
Employees must never share their passwords with another person.

3. CONFIDENTIAL DATA
Employees must not share confidential company information with unauthorised people.

4. GENERATIVE AI
Employees must not enter confidential company information into public AI tools.

5. USB DEVICES
Employees must not connect unknown USB devices to company computers.

6. REMOTE WORKING
Employees must use approved devices when working remotely.

7. LOST DEVICES
Employees should report lost company devices to the IT team.

8. SECURITY INCIDENTS
Employees should report security incidents as soon as possible.

9. PUBLIC WI-FI
Employees should use approved security protections when accessing company systems on public Wi-Fi.

10. SOFTWARE
Employees must not install unauthorised software on company computers.
